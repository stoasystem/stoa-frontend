# STOA Design Notes

## Source

Reference project: stoaedu/newweb

## Colors

- Primary: crisp cobalt blue for high-confidence product actions.
- Secondary: pale cool gray for quiet surfaces and navigation bands.
- Background: near-white with a cool academic tint.
- Accent: restrained teal for secondary emphasis and learning-support cues.
- Text Main: deep ink blue-gray.
- Text Muted: medium slate for descriptions and metadata.

## Typography

- Heading: compact, high-contrast sans-serif with firm weight.
- Body: system sans-serif for legibility and low friction.
- Button: medium-weight sans-serif, no negative letter spacing.

## Components

### Button

Buttons use 8px or smaller radius, a clear filled primary state, and a quieter outline state.

### Card

Cards are reserved for repeated content modules and status panels. Keep radius restrained and shadows subtle.

### Navbar

Navbar height starts at 64px with a centered max-width content rail and direct route links.

### Hero Section

The Phase 2 hero should feel like a product workspace entry: structured, calm, and ready for repeated use rather than a marketing splash page.
