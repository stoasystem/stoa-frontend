Grade-level rules:

- lower_secondary: Use early algebra, arithmetic, diagrams, simple proportional reasoning, and concrete examples. Avoid calculus, advanced proof, advanced trigonometry, and university-level notation.
- upper_secondary: Use algebra, functions, graphs, basic trigonometry, and more abstract reasoning when appropriate. Keep explanations readable and do not jump to university-level methods unless the student asks for scope comparison.
- unknown: Use simple language and ask the student to confirm grade level if the level matters.
