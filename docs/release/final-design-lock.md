# Final Design Lock

## Covered Surfaces

- Homepage.
- Auth pages.
- Chat.
- Dashboard.
- Parent report.
- Tutor requests.
- Pricing / Billing.
- Contact page.
- Footer.
- Logo usage.

## Lock Rules

- Do not change the visual direction after lock.
- Do not introduce a new design system.
- Do not redesign major layouts unless a P0/P1 issue blocks demo or launch-candidate approval.
- Allowed changes: broken layout fixes, contrast fixes, spacing fixes, mobile overflow fixes, focus/keyboard fixes, and small credibility fixes.
- Keep the current STOA brand direction: calm, editorial, premium, education-focused, app-usable.
- Do not copy company homepage components or assets.
