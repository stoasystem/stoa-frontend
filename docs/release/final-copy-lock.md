## Lock Rules

- Do not make casual copy changes after lock.
- Allow only typo, grammar, clarity, serious misleading-copy, and blocker fixes.
- Do not update English without checking German, French, and Italian.
- Do not reintroduce user-facing `demo`, `mock`, `test account`, `Codex`, provider, or development wording.
- Keep `Learning Assistant` for first-response learning help and `Professional teacher support` for teacher escalation.
- Keep parent-report language calm and non-alarmist.
- Keep pricing copy clear without implying real payment processing is live.
