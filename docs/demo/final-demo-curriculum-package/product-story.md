# Phase 30 Product Story

## Core Story

English:

STOA helps students practise step by step, ask for clear explanations when they get stuck, and receive professional teacher support when they need more guidance. Parents can follow the learning process through clear progress reports.

German:

STOA unterstützt Schüler beim schrittweisen Üben, erklärt schwierige Schritte verständlich und ermöglicht professionelle Unterstützung durch Lehrpersonen, wenn zusätzliche Hilfe nötig ist. Eltern behalten den Lernfortschritt klar im Blick.

French:

STOA aide les élèves à s'entraîner étape par étape, à recevoir des explications claires lorsqu'ils sont bloqués et à bénéficier de l'accompagnement d'un enseignant qualifié si nécessaire. Les parents suivent les progrès grâce à des rapports clairs.

Italian:

STOA aiuta gli studenti a esercitarsi passo dopo passo, a ricevere spiegazioni chiare quando incontrano difficoltà e a ottenere il supporto di un insegnante qualificato quando serve. I genitori possono seguire i progressi con report chiari.

## Product Logic

STOA is not only a place to ask questions. It is a guided learning platform:

1. Practice helps students begin actively.
2. Hints help them recover from mistakes.
3. Learning Chat explains the exact stuck step.
4. Teacher support adds human guidance when needed.
5. Parent Report makes the learning process visible.

## External Positioning

Use this language:

`STOA connects active practice, guided explanations, professional teacher support, and parent visibility in one learning flow.`

Avoid this language:

- `AI answer engine`
- `mock provider`
- `demo backend`
- `fake data`
- `automated tutor replacement`
