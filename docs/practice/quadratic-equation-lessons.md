# Quadratic Equation Lessons

## Unit Goal

Students recognize simple quadratic equations, factor small expressions, use the zero-product idea, and check two possible solutions.

## Lessons

1. Recognizing quadratic equations
2. Factoring simple quadratics
3. Solving factored quadratics
4. Checking two solutions

## Included

- x squared recognition
- simple factoring with small integer pairs
- zero-product property
- checking values in the original equation

## Excluded

- quadratic formula derivation
- discriminant discussion
- complex roots
- vertex formula
- graph transformations
- calculus

Quadratics in this demo should stay simple enough for a short guided walkthrough.
