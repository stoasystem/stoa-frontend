# Phase 12 Tutoring Center Onboarding

Tutoring center onboarding is a frontend demo for early partner conversations.

Routes:

- `/for-tutoring-centers`
- `/partnership/onboarding`

The onboarding form posts to `POST /partnership/interests` and falls back to a mock success response during local demo use.

Out of scope:

- CRM pipeline
- Tutor payroll
- Scheduling enforcement
- Production partner backend
