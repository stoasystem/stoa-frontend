## German Style Signals

### Headline Rhythm

Use short, confident headline units. German large headings should feel composed and editorial, not like full product explanations.

Preferred learning-platform pattern:

- `Lernen. Fragen. Verstehen.`
- `Klar lernen.`
- `Mehr Orientierung beim Lernen.`
- `Fortschritt verständlich machen.`

Avoid:

- long direct translations from English
- stacked compound nouns
- full subordinate clauses in hero titles

### CTA Style

Use short, familiar German actions.

Preferred:

- `Lernen starten`
- `Frage stellen`
- `Weiter`
- `Bericht ansehen`
- `Plan wählen`
- `Lehrperson fragen`
- `Kontakt aufnehmen`

Avoid:

- `Jetzt kaufen`
- `Lernplattform betreten`
- `Detaillierten Lernfortschrittsbericht anzeigen`
- `Professionelle Lehrpersonenunterstützung anfragen`

### Tone

The STOA German voice should feel:

- ruhig
- klar
- zuverlässig
- bildungsnah
- hochwertig
- nicht werblich

Use a parent- and institution-appropriate level of formality. Student-specific chat copy may be more direct and warm.

### Education Vocabulary

Preferred vocabulary:

- `Lernunterstützung`
- `Lernfortschritt`
- `Erklärung`
- `Lehrperson`
- `Begleitung`
- `Unterstützung`
- `Hausaufgaben`
- `Lernfragen`
- `Verständnis`
- `Orientierung`

Use `Nachhilfe` sparingly and intentionally. It is natural, but STOA’s platform copy should not reduce the product to traditional tutoring.

## Learning Platform Adaptation

### Homepage

The homepage can use the strongest brand rhythm. German hero title remains stacked and compact. Subtitle explains the complete service value.

### Register And Onboarding

Registration should use clear account-setup language. Avoid explaining internal role logic. Parent and teacher copy can use formal address; student copy can stay direct.

### Chat

Chat copy should be practical and short. Do not over-brand message states. Teacher-support copy should sound professional, not like an emergency escalation.

### Parent Report

Use supportive language. Do not create anxiety. Prefer phrases like `zeigt Fortschritte`, `kann weiter üben`, and `zusätzliche Unterstützung kann helfen`.

### Pricing And Billing

Use value language rather than sales pressure. Prefer `Plan wählen` and `Unterstützung durch Lehrpersonen` over buy-oriented or compound-heavy phrasing.

## Review Checklist

- Does the German sentence sound written in German first?
- Is the heading short enough for large typography?
- Is the CTA one clear action?
- Are long compounds avoided in buttons and card titles?
- Does the copy preserve STOA’s education-service tone?
- Is the meaning aligned with English, French, and Italian without requiring literal structure?
