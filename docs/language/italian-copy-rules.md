# Italian Copy Rules

Italian STOA copy should feel natural, warm, and clear without becoming vague or overly emotional.

## Tone

Italian copy should feel:

- friendly
- calm
- clear
- supportive
- education-centered

Avoid overly abstract slogans or literal English sentence rhythm.

## Headline Rules

Prefer:

- Studiare con più chiarezza.
- Imparare con il giusto supporto.
- Domande più semplici da affrontare.

Avoid:

- Una piattaforma intelligente completa per automatizzare il supporto allo studio.

Hero titles can be slightly longer than German, but should not exceed two comfortable lines.

## Subtitle Rules

Approved hero direction:

STOA aiuta gli studenti a fare domande, ricevere spiegazioni chiare e ottenere il supporto di un insegnante qualificato quando serve.

If the subtitle feels long, keep the hero title short and let section copy carry additional detail.

## CTA Rules

Prefer:

- Inizia a studiare
- Fai una domanda
- Chiedi a un insegnante
- Vedi il report
- Scegli un piano
- Continua

Avoid:

- Acquista ora
- Backup umano
- Backup dell’insegnante
- Supporto AI

## Layout Rules

For Italian UI:

- check CTA buttons on mobile
- allow natural line wrapping in cards
- avoid shrinking all typography because one label is long
- keep plan and billing copy concise

## Parent and Tutor Copy

Parent copy should sound supportive and practical. Tutor copy should present qualified teaching support as professional help, not as a fallback system.

## Phase 20 CTA and Layout Rules

Italian CTAs are often longer than English. Buttons should allow comfortable width, but copy should still stay direct.

Preferred full / short pairs:

- `Inizia a studiare` / `Inizia`
- `Fai una domanda` / `Domanda`
- `Chiedi a un insegnante` / `Chiedi`
- `Scegli un piano` / `Scegli`

## Phase 20 Refinement Rules

- Keep copy warm without becoming vague.
- Use natural Italian word order rather than literal English structure.
- Avoid long explanation inside card titles.
- Keep support and billing copy clear and practical.
- Use shorter labels on mobile when the full CTA crowds the button.
