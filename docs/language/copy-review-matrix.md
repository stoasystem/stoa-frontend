## Review Notes

- German hero copy uses explicit stacked title lines to avoid a long direct translation in large serif typography.
- French keeps natural sentence rhythm while avoiding overly abstract or formal marketing phrasing.
- Italian keeps CTA language warm and direct without exceeding expected button widths.
- Parent report copy stays non-alarmist: it talks about progress, practice, and next steps rather than deficits.
- Tutor copy presents teachers as professional support, not replacement or backup.
- Pricing and billing copy uses education value language and plan choice language instead of aggressive purchase wording.
