# Home V2 v6.4 Final Photography Contract

**Date:** 2026-07-06
**Scope:** Hero and Parent Confidence photography for `/home-v2`

## Top Standard

The image must help a Swiss middle-to-upper-income parent feel: this is a calm, credible, refined education product for my child, not a generic AI tool or stock tutoring service.

## Hero Photography Contract

### Accepted Direction

The Hero image should show a credible family-learning moment:

- Swiss/European plausibility without leaning on clichés.
- Parent and child learning together or near each other in a natural, warm home setting.
- Calm interior, natural window light, quiet wardrobe, and restrained color.
- A learning moment that feels unresolved-but-supported, not a staged success advertisement.
- Composition supports the current desktop tall frame and mobile landscape frame.
- Enough negative space or soft background to hold the proof panel overlay.

### Rejection Criteria

Reject candidates that show:

- AI-generated identifiable faces.
- Wrong-market family signal for the Swiss parent audience.
- Over-smiling commercial stock energy.
- Bright saturated interiors, loud props, or toy-like color.
- A laptop as the main visual story unless the human learning moment is clearly stronger.
- Classroom/school settings that imply a school endorsement.
- Tutor/teacher standing over a child as the main scene.
- Any obvious watermark, brand, school logo, readable private data, or licensing ambiguity.

### Crop Checks

- Desktop: must work in the current `lg:aspect-[6/7]` Hero frame.
- Mobile/tablet: must work in the current `aspect-[16/11]` / `aspect-[16/10]` frame.
- Faces/hands should not be cut awkwardly by the proof panel.
- The image must still read as warm and premium after the existing Home V2 image tone filter.

## Parent Confidence Photography Contract

### Accepted Direction

The Parent Confidence image should communicate that parents can understand learning progress without taking over:

- Parent is nearby or calmly engaged, not hovering.
- Child/student work remains the center of agency.
- The scene suggests progress visibility, review, or study rhythm.
- Warm but restrained interior: quiet neutrals, natural light, limited saturated color.
- Works with the existing weekly note overlay.
- Can be partial-face, back/side angle, or context-led if it improves privacy and elegance.

### Rejection Criteria

Reject candidates that show:

- Surveillance posture: parent looking over shoulder like monitoring.
- Parent correcting aggressively or taking control of the work.
- Generic laptop stock scene with bright orange/yellow interior.
- Smiling sales-photo energy.
- Wrong-market demographics or cultural context for Swiss-first positioning.
- AI-generated identifiable faces.
- Readable personal information, school names, trademarks, or brand marks.

### Crop Checks

- Must work in current Parent Confidence visual frame.
- Must allow the weekly note overlay without covering the most meaningful human gesture.
- Must not become face-dominant on mobile.
- Should improve over current `UNS-1758687126234`; otherwise current preview remains.

## Licensing And Source Checks

Every candidate must record:

- Source URL.
- License/source URL.
- Creator/vendor.
- Download URL if downloaded.
- AI/enhancement status.
- Whether identifiable people appear.
- Endorsement risk.
- Preview suitability and final suitability.

## Free Vs Paid Decision

Free-source candidates are acceptable only if they are clearly stronger than the current preview image. If not, keep the current preview image and recommend paid iStock or commissioned photography.

Paid candidates remain link-only until explicitly purchased and approved.
