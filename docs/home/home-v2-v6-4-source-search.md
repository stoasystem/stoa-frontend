# Home V2 v6.4 Source Search

**Date:** 2026-07-06
**Scope:** Final Hero and Parent Confidence photography

## Search Goal

Find source-clear photography that is stronger than the current `/home-v2` preview images for a Swiss middle-to-upper-income parent audience.

The goal was not to collect many images. The goal was to decide whether a free-source replacement is strong enough for final public use, or whether the page should move to paid/commissioned sourcing.

## Free-Source Findings

### Hero

`PEX-8121121` remains the strongest free preview candidate already in the repo. It has a real family-learning moment, sufficient resolution, and plausible European context. It still reads more like broad stock family learning than refined Swiss private-education photography.

`UNS-1549227082` is warm and family-centered, but the Christmas setting makes it seasonal and unsuitable as an evergreen public Hero.

`UNS-1585432959445` has a parent/child study scene, but the image is ordinary and less premium than the current Hero.

### Parent Confidence

`UNS-1758687126234` remains usable for preview after the current tone treatment. Its weakness is not source quality; it is visual language. The orange wall, laptop-centric story, and smiling stock energy reduce the high-end calm needed for final public use.

No free candidate in the current pool clearly communicates "parents understand progress without taking over" better than the current preview image.

## Paid-Source Findings

iStock remains the practical next procurement route because paid search can target Swiss/European family-learning imagery more directly than broad free-stock routes. Paid candidates must remain link-only until purchased and approved.

Recommended paid search filters:

- Geography/context: Switzerland, Europe, international school, home study.
- People: parent and child, side/back/partial-face acceptable, no exaggerated smile.
- Scene: warm natural light, quiet home interior, desk/table learning, paper or notebook visible.
- Mood: calm support, child agency, parent nearby, no surveillance posture.
- Composition: vertical/tall crop candidate for Hero, landscape with overlay-safe negative space for Parent Confidence.

## Decision

Do not download or implement new free assets in v6.4.

Keep current images for `/home-v2` preview. Do not approve them as final public homepage photography. Move final public readiness to paid iStock/Getty review or commissioned Swiss/European education photography.
