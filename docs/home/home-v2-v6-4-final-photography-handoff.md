## Paid/Commissioned Shoot Brief

### Hero

- Parent and child at home, naturally close but not posed.
- Learning material visible: notebook, worksheet, book, or tablet as secondary support.
- Natural window light, quiet interior, neutral wardrobe.
- Side/back/partial-face angles acceptable if they feel elegant and privacy-respecting.
- Must work in both tall desktop and landscape mobile crops.

### Parent Confidence

- Parent understands progress without taking over.
- Child agency remains central.
- Weekly review, study rhythm, or calm check-in moment.
- No surveillance posture.
- Overlay-safe negative space for the weekly note.

## Do Not Use

- AI-generated identifiable family or child faces.
- Generic bright stock interiors.
- Parent hovering over shoulder like monitoring.
- School logo/classroom imagery implying endorsement.
- Watermarked paid previews.
- Any image with unclear licensing or private readable information.
