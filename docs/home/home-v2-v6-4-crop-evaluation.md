# Home V2 v6.4 Crop And Page-Fit Evaluation

**Date:** 2026-07-06
**Scope:** Hero and Parent Confidence imagery for `/home-v2`

## Current Frame Requirements

### Hero

- Desktop uses a tall editorial image frame.
- Mobile and tablet use a shorter landscape frame.
- The proof panel overlays the image, so the main human gesture needs to survive partial cover.
- The image must remain calm after the Home V2 tone filter.

### Parent Confidence

- The image sits inside the Parent Confidence visual frame.
- The weekly-note overlay must not cover the central emotional gesture.
- Mobile should not become face-dominant or visually loud.
- The image should imply parent visibility without monitoring.

## Hero Evaluation

`PEX-8121121` works because the bodies lean into one shared learning moment and the laptop does not completely overpower the scene. It also supports the proof panel better than the other free candidates.

Weaknesses:

- The visual still reads like general stock home learning rather than carefully commissioned Swiss education photography.
- The bright window and casual home staging are acceptable for preview but not fully high-end.
- Desktop tall crop has to choose between the family gesture and surrounding negative space.

Decision: keep as preview; do not approve as final public Hero.

## Parent Confidence Evaluation

`UNS-1758687126234` works because it shows a parent and child together in a clear study moment and has a stable landscape ratio for the note overlay.

Weaknesses:

- The saturated orange wall pulls the section away from the restrained Apple-like tone.
- The laptop is too dominant for a nuanced parent-progress story.
- The smile and staging feel commercial rather than quietly confident.

Decision: keep as preview after the existing tone treatment; do not approve as final public Parent Confidence photography.

## Replacement Decision

No free candidate clearly improves both emotional meaning and visual quality. Replacing the current preview images would create churn without solving the final-public quality problem.

The right next move is a paid/commissioned source round with the Phase 262 contract, not another broad free-stock scrape.
