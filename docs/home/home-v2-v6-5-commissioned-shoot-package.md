# Home V2 v6.5 Commissioned Shoot Package

**Date:** 2026-07-06

## Shoot Objective

Create final public photography for STOA Home V2 that feels calm, high-end, Swiss/European, and emotionally precise: families understand learning more clearly without turning home study into pressure or surveillance.

## Required Scenes

### Scene 1: Hero Family Learning Moment

Purpose: homepage first impression.

Shot needs:

- Parent and child at a table, desk, or quiet home study area.
- Student work is visible: notebook, worksheet, book, or tablet.
- Parent is close and supportive, not taking over.
- The moment feels like the question is being understood.
- Horizontal and vertical framing options.
- Negative space for a proof panel.

Deliver:

- 3 final selects.
- 1 vertical/tall crop candidate.
- 1 mobile landscape crop candidate.
- 1 wider safety crop.

### Scene 2: Parent Confidence / Weekly Check-In

Purpose: show that parents can understand progress without intervening anxiously.

Shot needs:

- Parent and child near learning material.
- Parent may be reading a note, reviewing a notebook, or looking at a calm progress summary.
- Child agency remains visible.
- No surveillance posture.
- Clean overlay-safe area for weekly note UI.

Deliver:

- 3 final selects.
- 1 overlay-safe landscape crop.
- 1 detail crop with hands/notebook.
- 1 mobile-safe crop where faces do not dominate.

### Scene 3: Learning Detail Safety Set

Purpose: supporting future Home V2 visual polish without relying on more faces.

Shot needs:

- Hands writing.
- Notebook/worksheet detail.
- Soft desk light.
- Subtle paper texture.
- No readable private data.

Deliver:

- 6 detail selects.
- Mix of landscape, square, and vertical crops.

## Wardrobe

- Quiet neutrals: cream, off-white, grey, navy, muted burgundy, muted green.
- Avoid bright orange, neon colors, large logos, sportswear branding, slogan shirts.
- Clothing should look natural, not styled like a luxury fashion ad.

## Location

- Calm Swiss/European home interior, study room, dining table, or warm apartment.
- Natural window light preferred.
- Clean but lived-in: books, paper, notebook, soft wood, subtle textiles.
- Avoid school classrooms, branded institutions, toy-heavy rooms, and corporate office settings.

## Props

- Notebook, pencil, workbook, neutral laptop/tablet as secondary prop.
- No visible school names.
- No readable personal data.
- No brand logos.
- No fake dashboard UI unless designed and cleared by STOA.

## Releases And Rights

Required:

- Model releases for all identifiable people.
- Parent/guardian release for any minor.
- Location/property release if needed.
- Usage rights for STOA website, landing pages, social previews, investor decks, sales collateral, and future crop/optimization work.
- Right to crop, retouch, color-grade, and compress images for web.

## Delivery Spec

- RAW or maximum-quality originals.
- Color-corrected high-resolution JPEG/TIFF finals.
- Minimum 4000px long edge for final selects.
- Separate crops for Hero desktop, Hero mobile, Parent desktop, Parent mobile.
- Color profile: sRGB for web finals.
- File names must include scene, select number, orientation, and date.

## Art Direction Notes

The images should feel closer to Apple-like restraint and editorial education photography than SaaS stock. Let light and space carry the trust signal. Avoid over-explaining the product in the image.
