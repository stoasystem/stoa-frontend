# Home V2 v6.3 Asset Briefs

**Date:** 2026-07-06
**Scope:** `/home-v2` preview route only
**Goal:** Move Home V2 toward a cinematic, high-end Swiss-parent homepage without stock clutter or AI-tool cues.

## Global Art Direction

The page should feel like a quiet product story for Swiss family education:

- Editorial Luxury, not SaaS dashboard.
- Cinematic scroll, not feature-section stacking.
- Warm light and material detail, not decorative gradients.
- Real people where identity matters; non-face detail where privacy and restraint matter.
- Apple-like restraint and keynote composition, adapted to STOA and not copied.

## Hero

**Need:** Main family-learning image that makes the page immediately human and credible.

**Must have:**

- Swiss/European plausibility.
- Parent and child engaged in learning without looking staged.
- Calm interior, natural light, restrained wardrobe, no loud props.
- Crop flexibility for desktop portrait-ish frame and mobile landscape-ish frame.

**Avoid:**

- AI-generated faces.
- Over-smiling stock family scenes.
- Classroom or tutoring poses that imply a school endorsement.
- Laptop-dominant scenes that read as generic edtech.

**Current verdict:** `PEX-8121121` remains the best free preview Hero. Final public Hero still likely needs paid/commissioned sourcing.

## Learning Thread

**Need:** The mechanism is already visual. Asset support should be light: fine-line language, warm node glow, and optional study-detail references.

**Must have:**

- A line that feels gradually illuminated, not scanned.
- Small points/markers that feel alive through glow and shadow.
- No dashboard icons, no heavy SaaS illustrations.

**Current verdict:** No new photography required for the main Learning Thread. Use fine-line and motion language in Phase 260.

## Parent Confidence

**Need:** A calmer parent-learning visibility image.

**Must have:**

- Parent understands progress without hovering or monitoring.
- Warm but refined home context.
- Less orange/commercial interior than the current image.
- Child/student can be partial, turned away, or context-light.

**Avoid:**

- Surveillance framing.
- Parent looking over child's shoulder in a controlling way.
- Bright generic laptop stock.

**Current verdict:** Current `UNS-1758687126234` is preview-safe but the highest-priority replacement candidate.

## Trust

**Need:** Quiet evidence, proportion, and boundaries.

**Must have:**

- Non-front-face learning detail.
- Warm paper/desk/light material.
- Enough humanity without identity.
- Works with fine-line principles and privacy-by-restraint.

**AI allowed:** Yes, if non-identifiable and high-end: hand detail, paper, pencil, window light, notebook, abstract learning-line light. Must be labelled AI-generated.

**Current verdict:** The current trust/detail direction is strongest and should be used as the benchmark for other sections.

## Final CTA

**Need:** A calm close-out, not another explanation.

**Must have:**

- Dark editorial surface or quiet detail.
- Clear Start learning action.
- No new information burden.

**Current verdict:** Current CTA is strong. Optional texture/light polish only if it does not add clutter.

## Motion References

Use Apple WWDC/product launch material for:

- Chapter-like pacing.
- Large image plus sparse copy.
- Light revealing material.
- A product mechanism being slowly disclosed.
- Text as emotional marker, not explanation.

Do not copy:

- Apple product visuals.
- Apple logos/assets.
- Exact page compositions or keynote frames.
- Device mockups that imply Apple affiliation.

## Fine-Line / Icon Language

Use self-authored CSS/SVG line language where possible:

- Thin strokes.
- Small points and labels.
- Soft gold/burgundy light.
- No thick generic icon packs.
- No decorative icon grid.

External icon libraries are not needed for v6.3 unless a later phase proves a specific gap.
