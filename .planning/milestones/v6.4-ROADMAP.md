## Phase Details

### Phase 262: Final Photography Acceptance Contract

**Goal**: Hero and Parent Confidence photography criteria are precise before sourcing.
**Depends on**: Phase 261
**Requirements**: PHOTO-01, PHOTO-02
**Success Criteria**:
  1. Hero acceptance criteria cover Swiss/European plausibility, natural family learning, calm interior, crop flexibility, and no synthetic identity risk.
  2. Parent Confidence criteria cover progress clarity without monitoring, warmth without stock-commercial color, and note-overlay fit.
  3. Rejection criteria are explicit enough to avoid wasting time on wrong-market or generic stock candidates.
**Plans**: 262-PLAN.md
**UI hint**: yes

### Phase 263: Targeted Final Photography Source Search

**Goal**: Candidate search is purpose-led, source-clear, and suitable for final decision-making.
**Depends on**: Phase 262
**Requirements**: SRC-01, SRC-02, SRC-03, SRC-04
**Success Criteria**:
  1. Pexels/Unsplash free-source routes are searched against Hero and Parent briefs.
  2. iStock or comparable paid-source routes are recorded as link-only candidates with purchase rationale.
  3. Candidate ledger records source, license/source URL, creator/vendor, AI status, fit, risk, and download/commit decision.
  4. No paid preview or watermarked asset is committed.
**Plans**: 263-PLAN.md
**UI hint**: yes

### Phase 264: Crop And Page-Fit Evaluation

**Goal**: Shortlisted candidates are tested against the real Home V2 frames before any implementation decision.
**Depends on**: Phase 263
**Requirements**: CROP-01, CROP-02, CROP-03
**Success Criteria**:
  1. Hero candidates are evaluated against desktop tall frame and mobile landscape frame.
  2. Parent candidates are evaluated against current visual frame and proof-note overlay.
  3. Comparison explains candidate tradeoffs and whether current assets remain better than available free alternatives.
**Plans**: 264-PLAN.md
**UI hint**: yes

### Phase 265: Preview Asset Decision And Implementation

**Goal**: `/home-v2` receives only high-confidence source-clear asset changes.
**Depends on**: Phase 264
**Requirements**: IMPL-01, IMPL-02, IMPL-03
**Success Criteria**:
  1. If a stronger free candidate exists, it is optimized into `img/home-v2/preview/` and wired into `/home-v2`.
  2. If no stronger free candidate exists, current preview assets remain and the paid/commissioned recommendation is documented.
  3. Any page change is verified with responsive screenshots and keeps `/` unchanged.
**Plans**: 265-PLAN.md
**UI hint**: yes

### Phase 266: Final Asset Approval Handoff

**Goal**: v6.4 closes with a clear final photography decision and next-step recommendation.
**Depends on**: Phase 265
**Requirements**: APP-01, APP-02
**Success Criteria**:
  1. Final recommendation states approved preview asset, paid purchase candidate, commissioned shoot brief, or blocked.
  2. Handoff states whether Home V2 can move to switch-over preparation or must solve photography first.
  3. Lint/build/Home V2 E2E are run if code/assets changed.
**Plans**: 266-PLAN.md
**UI hint**: yes
