## Decision Notes

- Streaming uses `fetch` and browser `ReadableStream` rather than Axios.
- Token/chunk-level streaming state remains local to React.
- TanStack Query remains the canonical server-state layer after stream completion.
- Frontend continues to avoid direct model-provider SDKs and provider-specific environment variables.
