# Requirements: STOA Frontend v6.3

**Defined:** 2026-07-06
**Core Value:** `/home-v2` should feel like a quiet, trustworthy, high-end Swiss family education product story: cinematic, warm, restrained, visually precise, and close to public homepage quality without replacing `/`.

## v6.3 Requirements

### Full-Page Visual QA

- [ ] **VQA-01**: A Swiss middle-to-upper-income parent can scan `/home-v2` on mobile for 30 seconds and perceive a calm, credible, visually refined education product rather than an AI tool page.
- [ ] **VQA-02**: The full `/home-v2` page is audited across desktop, tablet, and mobile for hierarchy, whitespace, image dominance, text density, nav clarity, CTA clarity, and section rhythm.
- [ ] **VQA-03**: EN/DE/FR/IT layouts are checked for title length, button fit, paragraph rhythm, mobile wrapping, and obvious tone/layout red flags without attempting final native-language legal or marketing review.
- [ ] **VQA-04**: The audit classifies issues by severity and records which issues must be fixed in v6.3 versus deferred.

### Asset Readiness

- [ ] **AST-01**: Current `/home-v2` images and existing candidate assets are assessed by section: Hero, Learning Thread, Parent Confidence, Trust, and Final CTA.
- [ ] **AST-02**: New asset collection runs from explicit briefs rather than broad stock browsing, covering photography, texture/light detail, fine-line/icon language, and motion references.
- [ ] **AST-03**: People and family hero imagery prioritizes real traceable licensed photography; identifiable AI faces are excluded from people/family hero imagery.
- [ ] **AST-04**: AI-generated assets are allowed only for non-identifiable, non-front-face, high-end light, texture, desk, hand, line, or abstract learning detail use, and must be labelled as AI-generated in the asset ledger.
- [ ] **AST-05**: Downloaded/submitted assets are limited to source-clear candidates intended for `/home-v2` preview or the formal candidate pool; paid assets are recorded as links and purchase recommendations only.
- [ ] **AST-06**: Each shortlisted asset records source, creator/vendor where available, license/source URL, AI status, intended section, preview/final suitability, risk notes, and commit/download decision.

### Visual Implementation

- [ ] **VIS-01**: High-confidence image, texture, icon/line, or visual polish decisions are implemented directly in `/home-v2` when source, license, quality, and section fit are clear.
- [ ] **VIS-02**: Visual changes preserve the agreed Home V2 five-section IA: Hero, Learning Thread, Parent Confidence, Trust, and Final CTA.
- [ ] **VIS-03**: The page avoids generic AI/SaaS visual language, cheap stock feel, decorative clutter, heavy dashboarding, and repeated promotional claims.
- [ ] **VIS-04**: Navigation, Login, and Start learning remain visually easy to find while staying consistent with the floating premium island system.
- [ ] **VIS-05**: Responsive adjustments prevent narrow-screen image dominance, text overlap, crowding, or CTA ambiguity.

### Motion Direction

- [ ] **MOT-01**: Motion direction learns from Apple WWDC/product-keynote composition: cinematic scroll, large image/white-space rhythm, careful reveal, light, materiality, and chapter-like pacing without copying Apple assets or specific layouts.
- [ ] **MOT-02**: Implemented motion is restrained and warm: breathing light, section reveal, line illumination, or subtle material depth rather than scans, particles, heavy Lottie stacks, or visual noise.
- [ ] **MOT-03**: Motion uses transform and opacity, avoids continuous expensive scroll handlers, respects reduced motion, and remains acceptable on mobile.

### Readiness Handoff

- [ ] **RDY-01**: v6.3 produces a clear judgment on whether `/home-v2` is ready to prepare for replacing `/`, needs one more polish milestone, or is not ready.
- [ ] **RDY-02**: v6.3 does not replace `/`, does not purchase paid assets, does not bypass watermarks, and does not modify login/product backend behavior.
- [ ] **RDY-03**: Verification evidence includes lint, build, focused Home V2 E2E, responsive screenshots, asset ledger/references, and a readiness report.

## Future Requirements

| Requirement | Reason Deferred |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Actual replacement of `/` with `/home-v2` | Requires a dedicated switch-over milestone covering SEO, routing, old-home preservation, rollback, monitoring, and production risk. |
| Paid iStock or commissioned photography purchase | v6.3 can identify and recommend paid assets, but purchase and license approval remain outside this milestone. |
| Final native-language copy review | v6.3 checks layout fit and obvious tone issues; final native review should be a later localization milestone. |
| Full brand campaign or marketing launch package | v6.3 is homepage readiness, not a campaign system. |
| Heavy animation system or new motion library adoption | v6.3 should keep implementation light and maintainable unless a later milestone proves the need. |

## Out of Scope

| Item | Reason |
|------|--------|
| Replacing the public homepage route `/` | v6.3 only judges readiness and keeps `/home-v2` isolated. |
| Buying paid stock or commissioned photography | Budget, license, and final use approvals remain separate. |
| Submitting paid preview/watermarked images as page assets | This creates legal and quality risk. Paid assets are link-only until purchased. |
| AI-generated identifiable people or family hero imagery | The Swiss-parent homepage needs authentic trust and avoids synthetic identity risk. |
| Copying Apple assets, pages, logos, product visuals, or exact keynote frames | Apple is a composition and motion reference, not a source to clone. |
| Adding new homepage sections or changing the five-section IA | v6.3 is a final-quality pass, not an IA rewrite. |
| Backend, auth, quota, payment, registration, or role-dashboard behavior | The milestone is public homepage visual and asset readiness only. |
