## Phase Details

### Phase 267: Procurement Strategy And Licensing Guardrails

**Goal**: The team knows when to buy stock, commission photography, or accept temporary preview imagery.
**Depends on**: Phase 266
**Requirements**: PROC-01, PROC-02, PROC-03
**Success Criteria**:
  1. Procurement paths are explicit.
  2. License/release checks are documented for all relevant source types.
  3. Paid previews and watermarked images remain out of the repo.
**Plans**: 267-PLAN.md
**UI hint**: yes

### Phase 268: Final Photography Art Direction

**Goal**: Hero and Parent Confidence final photography can be briefed without re-litigating visual taste.
**Depends on**: Phase 267
**Requirements**: ART-01, ART-02, ART-03
**Success Criteria**:
  1. Hero art direction specifies the learning moment, setting, light, composition, and crop needs.
  2. Parent Confidence art direction specifies progress visibility without monitoring.
  3. High-end image traits and rejection rules are clear.
**Plans**: 268-PLAN.md
**UI hint**: yes

### Phase 269: Paid Stock Review Template

**Goal**: Paid stock review is structured enough to support a purchase decision.
**Depends on**: Phase 268
**Requirements**: STOCK-01, STOCK-02, STOCK-03
**Success Criteria**:
  1. iStock/Getty search routes and query variants are documented.
  2. Candidate scoring template records fit, license, release, crop, endorsement, and recommendation fields.
  3. Paid-stock rejection rules preserve the premium Swiss parent direction.
**Plans**: 269-PLAN.md
**UI hint**: yes

### Phase 270: Commissioned Shoot Package

**Goal**: A photographer or producer can execute the required image set.
**Depends on**: Phase 269
**Requirements**: SHOT-01, SHOT-02, SHOT-03
**Success Criteria**:
  1. Hero and Parent Confidence shot list is explicit.
  2. Wardrobe, location, model/release, props, light, and composition requirements are documented.
  3. Delivery specs support future frontend implementation and optimization.
**Plans**: 270-PLAN.md
**UI hint**: yes

### Phase 271: Approval Workflow And Implementation Handoff

**Goal**: The next implementation milestone has a precise intake checklist.
**Depends on**: Phase 270
**Requirements**: APPROVAL-01, APPROVAL-02, APPROVAL-03
**Success Criteria**:
  1. Candidate approval workflow is documented.
  2. Switch-over remains blocked until final image approval.
  3. Future implementation checklist is clear.
**Plans**: 271-PLAN.md
**UI hint**: yes
