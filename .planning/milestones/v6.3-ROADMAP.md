## Phase Details

### Phase 257: Home V2 Full-Page Visual QA

**Goal**: `/home-v2` is reviewed as one cinematic product story, not as isolated improved sections.
**Depends on**: Phase 256
**Requirements**: VQA-01, VQA-02, VQA-03, VQA-04
**Success Criteria**:
  1. Desktop, tablet/narrow, and mobile screenshots are captured for the full `/home-v2` page.
  2. EN/DE/FR/IT layouts are checked for title length, CTA fit, paragraph rhythm, and awkward wraps.
  3. The audit identifies severity-ranked visual, asset, responsive, motion, copy, and entry-point issues.
  4. The audit explicitly tests the top acceptance standard: a Swiss parent can scan mobile for 30 seconds and perceive calm, credible, refined education support rather than an AI tool page.
**Plans**: 257-PLAN.md
**UI hint**: yes

### Phase 258: Purpose-Led Asset Collection And Shortlist

**Goal**: Home V2 has a targeted asset shortlist and ledger based on section briefs rather than broad stock browsing.
**Depends on**: Phase 257
**Requirements**: AST-01, AST-02, AST-03, AST-04, AST-05, AST-06
**Success Criteria**:
  1. Each section has a precise asset brief and current asset verdict.
  2. New collection covers real photography for people/family hero use, non-face AI detail possibilities, texture/light material, fine-line/icon language, and Apple Keynote/product-page motion references.
  3. Downloaded assets are limited to traceable preview/candidate use; paid assets and brand references are recorded as links only.
  4. The asset ledger records source, creator/vendor when available, license/source URL, AI status, section fit, preview/final suitability, risks, and commit/download decision.
  5. Candidate choices reject cheap stock feel, wrong-market demographics, identifiable AI faces, endorsement risk, and surveillance framing.
**Plans**: 258-PLAN.md
**UI hint**: yes

### Phase 259: Asset Landing And Visual Polish

**Goal**: High-confidence v6.3 visual improvements are implemented in `/home-v2` without expanding scope.
**Depends on**: Phase 258
**Requirements**: VIS-01, VIS-02, VIS-03, VIS-04, VIS-05
**Success Criteria**:
  1. Approved preview assets or visual-material improvements are wired into the Home V2 implementation where certainty is high.
  2. Hero, Learning Thread, Parent Confidence, Trust, and Final CTA retain the agreed IA and improve as one coherent page.
  3. Nav/Login/Start learning remain clear, refined, and mobile-usable.
  4. Narrow viewports avoid image dominance, crowding, text overlap, and CTA ambiguity.
  5. Code changes remain scoped to Home V2 components/styles/assets and do not alter `/`.
**Plans**: 259-PLAN.md
**UI hint**: yes

### Phase 260: Cinematic Motion Direction And Implementation

**Goal**: Home V2 motion feels alive and warm without becoming loud, heavy, or template-like.
**Depends on**: Phase 259
**Requirements**: MOT-01, MOT-02, MOT-03
**Success Criteria**:
  1. A motion direction document captures Apple Keynote/product-page principles adapted to Swiss family education without copying Apple assets or exact layouts.
  2. Implemented motion uses restrained reveal, breathing light, line illumination, or material depth rather than scanning, particles, or heavy Lottie stacks.
  3. Motion uses transform/opacity, avoids expensive continuous scroll handlers, respects reduced motion, and works on mobile.
  4. The Learning Thread and surrounding sections feel connected but do not distract from content.
**Plans**: 260-PLAN.md
**UI hint**: yes

### Phase 261: Cross-Locale Verification And Readiness Handoff

**Goal**: v6.3 closes with technical proof, screenshot evidence, and a clear readiness judgment while keeping `/` unchanged.
**Depends on**: Phase 260
**Requirements**: RDY-01, RDY-02, RDY-03
**Success Criteria**:
  1. `npm run lint` passes.
  2. `npm run build` passes.
  3. `npm run test:e2e -- home-v2.spec.ts` passes.
  4. Responsive and cross-locale screenshots show no incoherent overlap, image dominance, text crowding, or broken key entry points.
  5. A readiness report states one of: ready to prepare switch after final asset approval, needs one more polish milestone, or not ready.
  6. The report confirms `/` is not replaced and identifies remaining asset, licensing, localization, SEO, and switch-over work.
**Plans**: 261-PLAN.md
**UI hint**: yes
