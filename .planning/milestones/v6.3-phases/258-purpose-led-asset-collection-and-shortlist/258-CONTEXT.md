**Gathered:** 2026-07-06

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Collect and score visual assets for `/home-v2` based on explicit section briefs. This includes real photography, existing downloaded candidates, non-face AI detail opportunities, texture/light material, fine-line/icon language, paid-stock search recommendations, and Apple Keynote/product-page motion references.

This phase does not purchase paid assets, does not bypass watermarks, does not commit broad reference images, and does not implement page changes.
</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

- People/family hero imagery prioritizes real traceable licensed photography.
- AI-generated imagery is allowed for non-identifiable, non-front-face high-end light, texture, desk, hand, line, and abstract learning detail.
- Apple WWDC/product launch pages are reference material for cinematic scroll, chapter rhythm, light, materiality, and reveal, not assets to copy.
- Only source-clear assets intended for `/home-v2` preview or the formal candidate pool should be committed.
- Paid iStock candidates remain link-only until purchase and license approval.
</decisions>
