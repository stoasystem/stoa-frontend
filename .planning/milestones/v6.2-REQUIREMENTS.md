# v6.2 Requirements: Home V2 Trust And Assurance Redesign

## Goal

Redesign the Home V2 Trust section into an artistic, warm, high-end assurance section that communicates service restraint, family respect, and reliability without repeatedly saying "Swiss" or turning privacy into a loud selling point.

## Scope

In scope:

- Rework `HomeV2TrustLayer` section structure, visual hierarchy, copy, and art direction.
- Replace ordinary feature-card grid behavior with an image-led assurance section and precision principle list.
- Introduce a strong art direction for a beautiful, warm, quiet image.
- Use a current placeholder only if it supports the direction; document if a better stock/paid/commissioned image is required.
- Preserve `/home-v2` isolation and existing product routes.

Out of scope:

- Paid image purchase unless separately approved.
- Replacing `/`.
- Legal privacy-policy writing.
- Claims about regulatory compliance not backed by policy.
- Repeating "Swiss" as a surface-level trust keyword.

## Decisions From Grill Session

- Do not repeatedly use "Swiss" in copy.
- Do not repeatedly emphasize privacy as a marketing claim; respect should feel built in.
- The section should feel Apple-like: warm, quiet, spacious, and precise.
- Trust is expressed through restraint, clear boundaries, calm language, and visual order.
- The section needs a very beautiful, artistic, warm image.
- Image direction: people are allowed, but avoid direct faces, camera-looking poses, and heavy tutoring/supervision imagery.
- Balance: family life warmth 60%, restrained education-service order 40%.

## Requirements

### TA-01 Trust Positioning

The section must communicate assurance through service behavior rather than slogans:

- Enough context, not excessive detail.
- Support with boundaries.
- Progress that can be discussed calmly.
- No inflated promises.

The copy must avoid:

- Repeated "Swiss" labeling.
- Repeated privacy claims.
- Surveillance, monitoring, or control framing.
- Overpromising instant improvement.

### TA-02 Image Art Direction

The section must include one high-quality, warm, artistic image direction:

- Mood: quiet, warm, refined, intimate.
- Light: natural morning/evening/window light preferred.
- Content: European/Swiss-compatible family environment, study table, paper, pencil, lamp, window, hands, partial figures, or spatial presence.
- Faces: optional, but avoid direct face-forward identity emphasis.
- Pose: no parent hovering over every step; no teacher-marketing stock pose.
- Role: image carries emotion and high-end atmosphere, not functional explanation.

### TA-03 Assurance Panel

The section must use a precision principle list instead of generic cards:

- 3-4 principles.
- Each principle has a short title and one restrained sentence.
- Use fine dividers, numbering, and whitespace.
- No icons are required.
- No card-heavy SaaS grid.

### TA-04 Copy Direction

Copy must be calm and minimal:

- Short title.
- Body no more than roughly 2-3 lines on desktop.
- Principle titles should be concrete, not abstract buzzwords.
- Principle descriptions should imply respect and boundaries without overusing privacy terminology.

Candidate principle directions:

- Enough context
- Support with boundaries
- Progress families can discuss
- No inflated promises

### TA-05 Responsive Layout

The section must work across:

- Desktop: image and principles form a composed editorial assurance layout.
- Narrow/tablet: image remains controlled and does not dominate the whole screen.
- Mobile: image and principle list stack without dense text or overlap.

### TA-06 Verification

Required verification:

- `npm run lint`
- `npm run build`
- `npm run test:e2e -- home-v2.spec.ts`
- Browser/screenshot smoke for desktop, narrow/tablet, and mobile Trust/Assurance views.

## Success Criteria

- The section no longer reads like a generic feature-card block.
- The image carries warmth and artistic quality.
- The text feels restrained and premium.
- Trust is communicated without shouting "Swiss" or "privacy".
- Existing Home V2 smoke tests pass.
