# v6.1 Requirements: Home V2 Parent Confidence Redesign

## Goal

Redesign the Home V2 Parent Confidence section so it communicates emotional relief for parents: they can stay close to the learning rhythm without anxiously taking over homework.

## Scope

In scope:

- Rework `HomeV2ParentConfidence` section structure, visual hierarchy, and copy.
- Reduce feature-explanation language and dashboard-like UI overlays.
- Use one restrained parent-facing note/proof surface instead of multiple pill labels.
- Preserve the current `/home-v2` preview route and do not replace `/`.
- Preserve EN/DE/FR/IT i18n plumbing and update localized copy keys as needed.
- Verify desktop, tablet/narrow, and mobile responsive layouts.

Out of scope:

- Final paid/commissioned image purchase.
- Replacing the public `/` homepage.
- Backend, auth, student, parent, teacher, or admin product workflows.
- New product claims about guaranteed improvement, surveillance, or instant outcomes.

## Decisions From Grill Session

- Parent Confidence should show a family state, not a feature list.
- The section should express: the parent no longer needs to anxiously intervene, but still feels informed enough to trust the next step.
- The image may be warmer and more concrete than the later Trust/Assurance section.
- Avoid copy such as dashboard, signals, visibility, AI, monitoring, or supervision-heavy language.
- UI proof should feel like a quiet weekly note or progress note, not a stack of SaaS feature pills.

## Requirements

### PC-01 Emotional Job

The section must make the parent-facing emotional promise clear:

- Parents can stay close without taking over.
- Parents can understand where support is needed without reading every step.
- The child keeps a sense of learning space and agency.

### PC-02 Visual Composition

The section must use a warm family-learning image or current best-fit placeholder from the Home V2 pool, with the following behavior:

- The image is not used as a generic full-bleed stock block.
- The image has controlled cropping across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- UI overlays are limited to one restrained proof/note surface.
- The visual does not imply surveillance, over-the-shoulder monitoring, or parent control.

### PC-03 Proof Surface

Replace the current three pill labels with one premium proof surface:

- Style: small weekly/progress note, paper slip, or quiet summary card.
- Content: one short parent-readable note.
- Tone: calm, practical, not technical.
- Must avoid feature-list density.

### PC-04 Copy Direction

Copy must be short, parent-first, and non-technical:

- Title should describe relief or calm proximity.
- Body should stay under roughly 2-3 lines on desktop.
- Proof note should be legible and not over-explain the product.
- EN/DE/FR/IT copy must keep the same semantic promise.

### PC-05 Responsive Quality

The layout must be checked at:

- Desktop around 1440px.
- Narrow/tablet around 900px.
- Mobile around 390px.

No image, proof note, title, body, or CTA may overlap or dominate the viewport incoherently.

### PC-06 Verification

Required verification:

- `npm run lint`
- `npm run build`
- `npm run test:e2e -- home-v2.spec.ts`
- Browser/screenshot smoke for desktop, narrow/tablet, and mobile Parent Confidence views.

## Success Criteria

- Parent Confidence no longer reads like a feature dashboard.
- The section clearly communicates parental reassurance without control or surveillance.
- Visual density is reduced.
- The proof note is useful but quiet.
- Existing Home V2 route smoke tests still pass.
