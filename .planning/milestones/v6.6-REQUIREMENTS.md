## Out of Scope

| Item | Reason |
|------|--------|
| Buying paid stock | Requires user budget and purchase approval. |
| Running a commissioned shoot | Requires external vendor/model/location coordination. |
| Replacing current preview images | No approved final files exist yet. |
| Replacing `/` | Explicitly blocked until a switch-over milestone. |
| Broad redesign after v6 | v6.6 closes the track rather than reopening design. |
