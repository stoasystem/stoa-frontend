# Home V2 Image Source Notes

**Date:** 2026-07-03
**Purpose:** Current source-positioning notes for the v2.8 Home V2 image and asset strategy milestone.

## Sources Reviewed

| Source | Current role for STOA | Notes |
|--------|-----------------------|-------|
| Pexels | First-pass free stock candidate source | Free use, attribution not required, but internal metadata should still record source/creator. Restrictions include endorsement, offensive portrayal, resale, redistribution, and trademark use. |
| iStock | Paid stock source, approval-gated | Standard/extended licenses; watermarked assets are only for comps/testing; AI Generator is a separate subscription path. |
| Magnific | Stock/enhancement research source, not default generator | Magnific combines stock assets and AI tools. Use stock assets first; treat AI generation/enhancement as explicitly recorded and approval-gated. |

## Strategic Decision

Home V2 should prioritize real licensed photography and stock images. AI-generated imagery is not the default because the homepage needs Swiss-parent trust, authentic education tone, and low uncanny-valley risk.
