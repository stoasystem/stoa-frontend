# UI Spec

## Legal Pages

- Use existing `PageContainer`, `PageHeader`, `Badge`, and `Button` components.
- Keep content readable, document-like, and visibly marked as pilot draft.
- Avoid marketing claims and avoid final legal certainty.

## Pricing Page

- Public route: `/pricing`.
- Show pilot, family, and school plan placeholders.
- State clearly that checkout, paid entitlement, and plan enforcement are not active.
- Provide navigation to registration for pilot access and billing placeholder for future
  paid-plan context.

## Billing Page

- Route: `/billing`.
- Page is a preparation surface only.
- State clearly that no payment method, invoices, or subscription enforcement exists.
- Link back to pricing and privacy.
