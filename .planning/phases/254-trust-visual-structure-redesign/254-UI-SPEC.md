# Phase 254 UI Spec

## Interaction Contract

- Section remains passive and scroll-revealed.
- Caption light breathes softly and respects reduced-motion.
- No hover-only content is required.

## Visual Contract

- Desktop: double-bezel image on the left; title, body, principles, and footnote on the right.
- Narrow/tablet/mobile: copy and principles come before the image to prevent image dominance.
- Principles use one list surface with fine dividers and numbers, not separate cards.
- The image caption should read as a learning signal rather than a dashboard or notification.
