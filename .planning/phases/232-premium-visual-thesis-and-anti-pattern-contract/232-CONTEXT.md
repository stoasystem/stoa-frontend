<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Visual Thesis
- Home V2 should feel like Swiss private-school / high-end education service first, with modern learning product evidence embedded quietly.
- The target impression is "Calm family confidence, editorial restraint, crafted product evidence."
- The first-screen visual job is parent confidence, not AI capability explanation.
- Student, tutor, parent app, and admin surfaces keep their own role-specific design context.

### High-End Skill Adaptation
- Use `high-end-visual-design` as craft input, not a literal template.
- Select Editorial Luxury plus Editorial Split as the main archetype.
- Use double-bezel containers for major image/product proof surfaces only.
- Keep button-in-button CTA treatment for `Start learning` and final primary actions.

### Anti-Patterns
- Reject generic SaaS feature grids, AI homework-solver styling, decorative gradient orbs, purple-blue tech gradients, one-note beige, and nested card stacks.
- Reject large scrolling blur, harsh shadows, generic gray borders, and hero copy in cards.
- Reject teacher-marketplace, student game, or compliance-badge wallpaper visuals.
- Avoid luxury decoration that is detached from learning proof.

### the agent's Discretion
The agent may choose exact wording inside the design document as long as it preserves the approved Swiss-parent, high-end education-service direction.

</decisions>
