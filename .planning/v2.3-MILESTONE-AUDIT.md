## Product Boundary Audit

Passed. User-facing copy and docs keep v2.3 as frontend/mock classroom UI. The implementation does not claim real WebRTC, real video provider connection, production scheduling, recording, transcription, attendance, billing, calendar sync, screen share, parent observer access, or production whiteboard support.

- Real tutor scheduling availability, matching, and notifications.

- Recording, transcript, replay, AI-generated summaries, and consent workflows.
- Production whiteboard/drawing engine and real screen sharing.
- Parent observer mode and admin classroom operations.

## Closeout

No blocking gaps found. The milestone is ready to archive and can serve as the frontend basis for future video-provider/backend classroom work.
